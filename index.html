Returns & Exchange
Process Oversight

A formal overview of the return and exchange workflow, governing policy, shipping charges, commercial terms and outstanding actions for management review.

View processOpen items

Executive summary

Customers initiate every return or exchange through the Khatam Returns Centre (khatam.com.my/pages/returns-portal), powered by PostCo and integrated with Shopify.

Return window

10 days

From the date of order fulfilment. Requests outside the window are ineligible.

Refund methods

2 options

Original payment method, or store credit issued as a Shopify gift card with no expiry date.

Return channels

3 routes

Free label (damaged or incorrect), paid label (other reasons), or free drop-off at a Khatam store or HQ.

Process flow

The customer journey is common up to the point of selection, after which the return and exchange paths diverge.

Return path

  1. Open the Returns CentreCustomer enters the order and selects the items to return.
  2. Eligibility check DecisionWithin 10 days of fulfilment? If not, the request is declined.
  3. Select return reason and methodDamaged or incorrect: free label. Other reasons: paid label. Alternatively, free drop-off.
  4. Item sent or dropped offShipped via Ninja Van, or handed over at a Khatam store (excluding booths) or HQ.
  5. Item received at KhatamCondition and return reason are verified by the operations team.
  6. Refund issuedTo the original payment method, or as a gift card store credit.

Exchange path

  1. Submit exchange requestItems must be unworn, unwashed, with all tags attached.
  2. Availability check DecisionIs the original item available in the required size?
  3. Same item, or alternativeSize changes and confirmed manufacturing defects are accepted; exchanges are generally limited to the same item.
  4. Price difference DecisionCostlier alternative: customer pays the difference via Shopify Payments. Cheaper: no cash refund.
  5. Exchange approvedReplacement fulfilled from stock.
  6. Delivery or collectionShipped at RM8 or RM15, or collected at HQ with fees waived manually.

Exchange conditions

Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with tags attached. Exchanges are limited to size changes and manufacturing defects confirmed by KHATAM®, and are generally limited to the same item. If the original is unavailable, the customer may select an alternative and pay any price difference. No cash refund is given where the replacement costs less.

Configuration requirement

Exchange delivery rates rely on a Shopify shipping profile named exactly PostCo Returns, with zones for Peninsular and East Malaysia and no weight or order-value conditions. PostCo’s own Exchange Shipping option must be disabled.

Conditions cannot be displayed above the Additional Comments field. The recommended placement is the Returns Centre landing page disclaimer, so customers see them before initiating a request.
